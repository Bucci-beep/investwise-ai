EXPERIMENT 4 — D5 FROZEN SENTENCE-TRANSFORMER REPRESENTATION

Purpose
-------
Test whether a pretrained contextual sentence representation improves D5
classification, particularly the compositional and contextual-attribution
weaknesses identified in Experiments 3 and 3B.

Models
------
Baseline:
- Existing BoundedTextClassifier
- Word TF-IDF (1,2-grams)
- Logistic Regression

Contextual candidate:
- sentence-transformers/all-MiniLM-L6-v2
- Frozen pretrained encoder
- 384-dimensional normalized sentence embeddings
- Logistic Regression
- No transformer fine-tuning

Logistic Regression settings:
- max_iter=1000
- random_state=42
- class_weight="balanced"

Training data
-------------
Intent:
- 120 D5 rows (V1 + V2 augmentation)
- clarity: 96
- undecided: 12
- confusion: 12

Option:
- 96 clarity rows
- 12 examples for each of 8 D5 options

No development-validation or stress-test examples were used for training.

CONCLUSION
----------
A frozen general-purpose sentence-transformer representation did not improve
D5 option classification.

It modestly improved intent classification, but substantially reduced option
performance and performed particularly poorly on multiple-source composition
and competing-source attribution.

The result does not support replacing the existing word TF-IDF representation
with frozen MiniLM embeddings.

Combined with Experiments 1-3B, the evidence suggests that the remaining D5
weakness is not simply insufficient surface-level or generic semantic
representation. The task requires explicit handling of relationships between
financial sources, including whether a mentioned source actually funds living
costs and whether multiple valid source categories jointly contribute.

This experiment does not establish that all transformer approaches would fail.
The encoder was frozen and was not adapted to the D5 task.

LIMITATIONS
-----------
- Training and evaluation data are synthetic/authored development data.
- The development and stress sets have already been observed during model
  development and are not independent final validation.
- Stress-test accuracy must not be interpreted as real-world accuracy.
- No transformer fine-tuning was performed.
- No conformal calibration or coverage evaluation was performed.
- No final test set was used.
- Results apply to this specific frozen MiniLM configuration and should not be
  generalized to transformer models as a whole.
